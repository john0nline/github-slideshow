title: "Creating your first skill"
---

1. **Do the task once in a chat** and refine it until the result is right
2. Ask: **"Turn what we just did into a skill"**. Claude drafts the SKILL.md
3. **Save it as a zip** of the skill folder and **upload it** in Settings, under the skills section
4. **Test it** in a new chat: "Here are my notes from the Acme call"
5. **Improve it** whenever the output isn't quite right

<small>Custom skills need a paid plan with code execution turned on. On Team and Enterprise plans, your admin may also share skills with everyone</small>
