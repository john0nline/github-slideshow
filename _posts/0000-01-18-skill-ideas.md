| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Account brief** | Researches a company and checks it against your ideal customer profile |
| **Call prep** | Builds a brief: attendees, history, open questions |
| **Call recap** | Writes the recap email, internal summary and CRM updates |
| **Objection coach** | Role-plays a buyer and scores your answers |
| **Weekly wrap** | Summarizes what closed, moved and slipped |

Start with the task you repeat most. Most AEs start with **call recap**
