Create one project per area you return to often:

| Project | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Pipeline and forecast** | Deal list exports, forecast notes, your quota and targets |
| **Account: Acme** | Call notes, stakeholder map, close plan, emails |
| **Prospecting** | Ideal customer profile, outreach templates, sequences |
| **Product and competition** | Product sheets, battlecards, case studies |

**Tip:** smaller, focused projects beat one giant "Work" project, because Claude gets the right context, not everything
