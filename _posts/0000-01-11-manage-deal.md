- **Deal review:** "What's missing to move this deal to the next stage?"
- **Stakeholder map:** "Who have we not spoken to yet? Am I single-threaded?"
- **Close plan:** a dated mutual action plan you can share with the customer
- **Competition:** "They're also looking at a competitor. How do we position against them?"
