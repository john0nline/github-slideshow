title: "Teaching Claude on purpose"
---

Don't wait for Claude to notice. Tell it directly:

- "Remember that my territory is the Western Cape and Namibia"
- "Remember that I prefer bullet points over long paragraphs"
- "Forget what I said about the old pricing. It's no longer valid"
- At the end of a good chat: **"What should you remember from this conversation?"**

**Correct it when it's wrong:** "That's out of date. Thandi was promoted to COO"
