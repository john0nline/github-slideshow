**Memory:** as you chat, Claude saves useful facts as short topics, such as your role, your accounts and how you like to work

**Chat search:** on paid plans, Claude can look back through earlier conversations when you refer to them ("What did we decide about Acme's pricing?")

**Scope:**
- Memory from chats **outside projects** is used everywhere
- Each **project has its own memory**, used only inside that project
- Projects don't read each other's memory

<small>Features and settings vary by plan, and on Team and Enterprise plans your admin controls them</small>
