A good request has four parts:

| Part | Example |
|---|---|
| **Context** | "I'm an AE selling to mid-market logistics companies" |
| **Task** | "Draft a follow-up email after today's discovery call" |
| **Input** | Paste the call notes or transcript |
| **Format** | "Under 150 words, with 3 clear next steps" |
{: style="font-size:0.8em"}

Then **iterate**: "Make it warmer", "Cut it in half", "Add a question about budget"
