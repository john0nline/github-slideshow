title: "Staying in control of memory"
---

- **Review and edit** what Claude remembers in Settings
- **One chat without memory:** turn off Memory in the **+** menu before your first message
- **Incognito chats** aren't saved and aren't searched later
- **Keep a project separate:** turn off "Use account memory" on the project
- **Sensitive topics**, such as health, are left out unless you opt in

> Check your memory once a month, like cleaning up your CRM
