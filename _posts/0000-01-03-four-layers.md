| Layer | What it holds | Where it applies | Who writes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Profile preferences** | Your personal rules and style | Every chat | You |
| **Memory** | Facts learned as you chat | Every chat, or one project | Claude, and you can edit it |
| **Projects** | Instructions and files for one area of work | Chats inside that project | You |
| **Skills** | Step-by-step playbooks for repeatable tasks | Any chat where the task comes up | You, with Claude's help |

This deck covers each one, then shows how they work together
