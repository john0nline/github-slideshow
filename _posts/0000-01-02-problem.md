title: "The problem with a blank chat"
---

Every new chat can feel like briefing a new hire from scratch:

- "I sell to mid-market logistics companies…"
- "Keep emails under 150 words, and never quote pricing…"
- "Acme's champion is the VP of Ops, and the CFO is skeptical…"

**The fix:** store that context where Claude will find it again

> Explain it once. Reuse it every day.
