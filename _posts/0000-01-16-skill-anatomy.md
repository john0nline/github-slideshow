# Call recap
1. Find the customer's pains, goals and objections, quoting their words
2. Write the recap email: thank them, summarize, list next steps
   with owners and dates. Under 150 words
3. Write a 3-bullet internal summary for my manager
4. List CRM updates as before → after. Don't apply them
5. Flag anything I promised that needs follow-up</code></pre>

The **description** matters most: it's how Claude decides when to use the skill
