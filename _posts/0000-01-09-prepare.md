- **Call brief:** who's attending, their roles, what happened last time
- **Discovery questions:** "Suggest 5 questions to uncover their onboarding pain"
- **Practice:** "Play a skeptical CFO. Push back on price, and I'll respond"

**Tip:** role-play is one of the fastest ways to sharpen your objection handling
