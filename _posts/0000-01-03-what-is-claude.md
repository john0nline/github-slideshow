title: "What is Claude?"
---

An AI assistant made by **Anthropic** that can read, write, research and reason with you

- **Great at:** summarizing, drafting, analyzing, structuring messy information
- **Works best with context:** the more you give it, the better it gets
- **Not a source of truth:** check facts, numbers and names before they reach a customer

> Think of it as a sharp new teammate: fast and capable, but it needs a good brief
