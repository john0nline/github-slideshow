Connectors let Claude work with your real data, not only what you paste in

- **Email and calendar:** prep meetings, find threads, draft replies
- **CRM (e.g. Salesforce, HubSpot):** read deals, suggest updates
- **Call recorders (e.g. Gong):** pull transcripts, find quotes
- **Docs and drive:** find decks, case studies, proposals

**Skills** are ready-made playbooks for common jobs, such as call prep or a pipeline review

<small>Your admin decides which connectors and skills are available</small>
