title: "Part 4: What is a skill?"
---

A saved playbook that teaches Claude **how** you do a specific task

- A folder with a **SKILL.md** file: a name, a description, and step-by-step instructions
- Can include **templates and examples**, such as your recap format
- Claude **loads it automatically** when a request matches the description
- Works **in every chat and every project**, so you set it up once

> Projects hold **what you know**. Skills hold **how you work**.
