title: "3. Meet and follow up"
---

Paste the transcript or your rough notes and ask for:

- A **recap email** to the customer, with next steps and owners
- A short **internal summary** for your manager or team channel
- **CRM updates:** stage, next step, close date, key risks
- **Objections raised**, and suggested responses

**Tip:** always read the recap before sending. You were on the call, Claude wasn't
