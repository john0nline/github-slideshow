- **Account research:** "Give me a brief on Acme: what they do, recent news, likely priorities"
- **Fit check:** "How well does Acme match our ideal customer profile? Here it is: …"
- **Outreach:** "Draft a 3-email sequence to their Head of Ops, based on this research"

**Tip:** give Claude your value proposition once, then reuse it in every prompt
